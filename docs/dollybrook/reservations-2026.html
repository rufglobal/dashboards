07 · Method & data quality

What is counted, and what was left out

Definitions

  • Room revenue - the Total Nightly field: accommodation only, before cleaning, fees and tax. Gross booking value including all of those is -.
  • Room-night - one cabin occupied for one night. A whole-resort buyout consumes nine per night.
  • ADR - room revenue ÷ room-nights sold. RevPAR - room revenue ÷ 3,285 available cabin-nights. RevPAB - room revenue ÷ 16,790 available bed-nights.
  • Occupancy - room-nights sold ÷ available, counted night by night so month-end stays split correctly.
