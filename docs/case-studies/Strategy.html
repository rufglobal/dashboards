Goal (purpose of strategy)

  • to win (define “winning”)

Strategy

  • General role: to develop a set of tactics and operations that holistically allows you to “win”

4 Key Questions

  • MARKET: Where do we compete? What market are we in?
  • UVP: What unique value do we bring in order to win? Why would our customers choose our services instead of competition
  • RESOURCES: What resources and capabilities we have  to deliver that value: exceptional human capital, superior technology, unrivaled network connections, unique reputation?
  • MOAT: How do we sustain our unique abilities? How do we continue to win over time?

What makes us better than the competition?

  • Actually, we are alone
  • This should make it easier for us to open the market, but in fact, it’s quite the opposite: we need to “create” the market, as an innovator. Clients do not yet understand the value of a new product
  • This is why it’s super important to keep things simple

What is the change we seek to make?

Process

  • Start discussing strategy in December of previous year
  • First meetings of the year: do a needs assessment
    • evaluate where the team thinks we need to improve
    • ask the board to submit their written feedback about where we need to improve
  • Craft and review the budget for new year
  • Establish desired targets, goals and projects for the year (be clear, very-very clear)
