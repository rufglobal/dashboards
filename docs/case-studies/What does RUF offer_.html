What does RUF offer?

​

Key terms:

  • clearing house for nonprofits
  • we offer exposure and legitimacy to validated projects and community partners
  • a “gofundme“ for Romanian projects (with tax-deductible benefits)

Tax Deductibility

RUF will be a 501c3 non-profit, making your donations tax-deductible in the United States. Through us, you can make tax-deductible donations to eligible nonprofits in Romania or even to small initiative groups or organizations in the United States (e.g. movie festival).

DUE DILIGENCE

Our thorough partner/project selection and evaluation due-diligence process guarantees only the most efficient and impactful projects are being funded.

Zero costs

RUF’s board of directors, business sponsors and members fully underwrite RUF’s operating costs, which means that 100% of every donation goes directly to the selected project

TranspArency

All project donations are deposited into a separate bank account. We will always present audited books and yearly reports.

Enhanced Focus

We believe that focusing on a reduced number of issues will ultimately make more of an impact than scattering small grants across a wide range of interests, at the same time saving time, administrative costs, and piles of paperwork.

Greater Impact

We work closely and offer full support to a select group of non-profits, connecting them with Romanian diaspora professionals that can bring extensive knowledge and vast connection networks in their fields, fully engaging them in the process.

Increased Convenience

RUF identifies and selects the most worthy causes in Romania and in the Romanian diaspora. By lowering the time and costs of research for donors, we actually encourage them to give more.
