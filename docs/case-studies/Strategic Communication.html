Tactics - What specific activities do we plan to execute? 

  • Strategic Partnerships with US organizations on Projects  (do they have to be only Romanian NGOs?)
  • Self-generated messages:
    • Internal: emails, memos, letters, newsletter, etc. 
    • External: newsletter, brochures, annual report, flyers, website, social media, press releases, etc. 
  • Strategic partnerships:
    • With local/national business, with other nonprofits, with local/state government, with international organizations
  • Programs and Events:
    • Educational programs, workshops, seminars, receptions, sponsorships, cultural events, community events, exhibits, festivals, etc.
  • Media relations and Paid Media:
    • Local/State print, radio, TV

Evaluation - How will we evaluate the effectiveness of our plan?

  • Quantitative
    • Google analytics, click-thrus & opens, social media feedback
    • Readership: e-newsletter sign-ups, print publications
    • Events
    • Campaigns
    • Amount fundraised
    • Membership/Angels count
  • Qualitative
    • What message worked?
    • Are there new audiences?
    • What is working and what needs to change?
