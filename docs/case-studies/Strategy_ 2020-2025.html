What is RUF 

  • A vibrant community for sustaining a strong and connected Romanian Diaspora in North America
  • A fundraising platform dedicated to building and supporting the Romanian  Diaspora nonprofit sector
  • A non-governmental, non-political, non-religious voluntary and philanthropic organization
  • A way for the Romanian diaspora to express their love for Romania by taking action and investing in the country’s future

To be clear define the platform as concept; Platform (usually meaning = online and offline fundraising channels)
