Dragos comments about strategy RUF / Global Romania 20250123

​

Context: 

  • the Romanian Diaspora is formed of local communities with a very wide level of development (informal ad-hoc gatherings to established community organizations), but there is a significant part of the diaspora, especially professionals, that is not connected with peers abroad (main reasons: decided to focus on their integration in the host country, avoidance of other Romanians or luck of opportunities to meet fellow Romanian)
  • the majority of the communities are developed as religious communities or as professional networks 
  • the majority of Romanian Diaspora associations are responding to the specific needs or aspirations of the members with a local/regional focus (e.g. preserve&promote Romanian heritage, social activities, representation with authorities in the host country, youth connection with Romania)
  • The communities started to interact at regional, transnational level and are exploring collaboration ways (partnership, federation, forum)
  • Limited support and coordination with the Romanian authorities (embassies, consulates or central government), but relations are improving

RUF

  • RUF is one of the most successful Diaspora community organizations that started in Chicago and has the potential to expand across US as well as globally
  • RUF model for community engagement found the right balance between stakeholders. It offers a wide range of options for participation - event participants, member, volunteer, founding angels. 
  • The capacity to support paid stuff allows for sustained activity. It allowed it to move from a fundraising platform to design and run programs and events (Excellence Center, Trips to Romania, RoSummit, ROCO community center Chicago)
  • Its activities attracted interest from others outside Chicago thus created the premises to serve other communities beyond Chicago. Other communities are interes
  • turned into into an ecosystem by mobilizing other organization and communities into its programs (e.g. new communities included in the excellence center, new organizations using the ROCO community center, new organizations partnering to organize the RoSummit) 
  • RUF is built a strong data driven organization that is able to leverage its members’ interests, skills and knowledge to drive impact.

​

GlobalRomania

  • Scale RUF’s local/regional success in building a striving global Romanian diaspora through engaging in collaborative projects, programs and events 
  • Build the global community with a focus on individuals’ participation and their willingness to engage in collaboration with peers in a win-win model
  • Provide a blueprint and support to build strong interconnected local community groups/organizations that can better serve their community members
  • Engage professionals from diaspora on several key areas (community development, healthcare, education, business) to enhance and make relevant the power of connectivity; these will be the pillars to support the global community 
  • Provide the connectivity between individuals and communities that drive transfer of knowledge and best practices, creates, promoted and match opportunities (at all levels from individual level to philanthropy and business ventures)   
  • Accelerate the connection with the support of technology solutions (online community, AI matchmaking)
  • Provide existing organizations the possibility to join GR to better serve their members
  • If Global Romania stays under RUF, RUF’s role will need to transition to serve the global Romanian community and ROCO/RUF US to take over Chicago or US specific projects and programs
