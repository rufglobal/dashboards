Mihai’s TODOs in 2021

1) Open the Chicago Community Center

  • The center acts as a “hook”, it allows us to do the “ask” (go to people and convince them to become members/founding angels)
  • I can help rally the entire Chicago community to raise the buildout money (40 x 1k)
  • We establish a “Romanian Community Center Fund of Chicago”, for people to donate to monthly
  • The community would “own it”, we would establish a “Council/Board” so they understand it’s theirs (elected every year)
  • The Board decides programs, projects, how to spend the money
  • We’d start with 3 zoom calls: start with 30 people (artists, community leaders)
  • Alina Cumpan (Chi) is a great leader and she would love this project, she would totally drive it

2) Global “Romanian Diaspora Awareness” campaign

  • Doubles as a fundraiser for Magic: “Helping Hands”
  • We engage the Federation and partner with Reteaua de Solidaritate (RDS) and Retea Cercurile Donatorilor (RDFN), Andra Martinescu (UK), 10 August
  • Every organization that joins the effort is a full partner and gets recognition
  • Precursor to a global Romanian Diaspora Day

3) Via Transilvanica

  • install/inaugurate VT milestone in Chicago
  • convince an important American figure to join the VT inauguration in Romania
